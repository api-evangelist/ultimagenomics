# Ultima Genomics

> Ultima Genomics develops high-throughput, cost-effective DNA sequencing platforms and solutions. Its UG200 Series sequencer targets population-scale genomics across germline, oncology, single-cell, proteomics, and epigenomics applications. The company publishes open bioinformatics tooling and AWS HealthOmics workflows rather than a hosted developer REST API.
